Weekly Rhythm

📅 Monday — Preparation

  • Plan your listings
  • Post ads early morning
  • Follow up old leads

📞 Tuesday — Lead Focus

  • Reply leads fast
  • Arrange viewings
  • Record inquiry notes

🏡 Wednesday — Viewing Day

  • Prepare scripts
  • Arrive early
  • Send follow-up messages

🔥 Thursday — Follow Up

  • Call warm leads
  • Send updated listings
  • Overcome objections

📝 Friday — Closing Day

  • Prepare booking forms
  • Assist loan submissions
  • Update your pipeline

🌱 Weekend — Growth

  • Learn new skills
  • Join team coaching
  • Shoot content
