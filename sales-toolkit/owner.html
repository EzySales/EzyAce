Owner Approach

Scripts and messages for approaching owners, handling objections, requesting exclusive listings, and negotiating price.

💡 Tone Tips

  • Always sound helpful, not desperate
  • Never lie about having buyers if you don't
  • Keep first messages under 3 sentences
  • Match the owner's tone — formal if they're formal, casual if they're casual

1 • First WhatsApp Message to Owner

Beginner-friendly 
Hi, good day! I'm [Your Name], a Registered Real Estate Negotiator (REN [number]) under [Agency Name].

I noticed your property at [address / project] and I currently have buyers actively looking in this area.

May I know if the unit is still available for sale? I'd love to arrange a quick viewing if possible. 🙏

Send this as your opening message to a new owner lead. Keep it short — owners are more likely to reply to a quick, low-pressure message than a long pitch.

2 • Cold Call Opening Script

Beginner-friendly 
"Hi Mr/Mrs [Name], good afternoon. My name is [Your Name] from [Agency Name].

I'm calling because I have a few buyers looking for a property similar to yours at [location]. Are you currently open to offers, or have you already listed it with another agent?"

Use for cold calls to owner leads (FSBO or expired listings). Speak slowly and pause after the question — let the owner respond instead of rushing into your pitch.

3 • Follow-Up After No Response

Beginner-friendly 
Hi [Owner Name], just following up on my earlier message 🙏

I still have serious buyers interested in properties around [area]. Let me know if your unit is still available, or if you've already sold it — no worries either way!

Send this only if there's no reply after 2–3 days. Don't follow up more than twice without a response — respect their silence and move on to other leads.

4 • Owner Says "I Already Have an Agent"

Use with caution 
No worries at all — sounds like you're already in good hands! 🙏

If it's alright with you, may I just stay in touch in case things don't work out with your current agent, or if you're ever open to widening your buyer reach with a second listing? I won't step on anyone's toes — just happy to be a resource if you need one down the road.

Use this the moment an owner mentions existing representation. Never criticize the other agent — staying respectful keeps the door open for a future co-broke deal or referral.

5 • Owner Says "Your Commission is Too High"

Use with caution 
I understand commission is an important consideration, and I want to make sure you're getting real value for it — not just a number.

My fee covers professional photography and marketing across multiple platforms, pre-qualifying serious buyers so your time isn't wasted, skilled negotiation to get you the best possible price, and handling all the paperwork and legal coordination through to completion.

Most owners find that a well-marketed, well-negotiated sale nets them more in the end than going with a discount agent. Happy to walk you through exactly how I'd market your property — would that help?

Don't get defensive or drop your rate immediately. Walk through the value first — most owners push back on commission because they haven't yet seen what they're actually paying for.

6 • Asking for Exclusive Listing Agreement

Use with caution 
Hi [Owner Name], thank you for trusting me with your property so far.

I'd like to propose something that usually gets better results: an exclusive listing for [1–3] months. This means I can invest fully in professional marketing for your unit, you'll only need to deal with one point of contact instead of juggling multiple agents, and in my experience exclusive listings tend to sell faster since buyers see one consistent price and message instead of conflicting ads.

Would you be comfortable moving forward with an exclusive agreement? I'll handle all the marketing, viewings, and negotiations for you at no upfront cost.

Best used after 1–2 positive interactions with the owner, once some trust is established. Lead with the benefit to them, not just the convenience for you.

7 • Price Negotiation with Owner

Use with caution 
Hi [Owner Name], I've been actively marketing your property and gathering feedback from buyers and recent transactions in the area.

Based on recent comparable sales — for example [comparable property] sold for RM[amount] and [comparable property] for RM[amount] — buyers are currently pricing units in [area] slightly below your current asking price of RM[amount].

I want your property to sell, not just sit on the market. Would you be open to adjusting the price to around RM[suggested price] to align with what buyers are currently willing to pay? This could significantly increase interest and help us move toward a sale faster.

Use when the listing has gone quiet for a few weeks with little buyer interest. Always bring 2–3 real comparable sales — data removes the emotion from the conversation.
