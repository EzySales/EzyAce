Buyer Follow Up

Templates for enquiries, viewings, bookings, loan updates, SPA process, and referrals.

1 • Replying to a New Enquiry

Hi [Buyer Name], thank you for your interest! 😊

The unit at [project/location] is priced at RM[price], [bedrooms] bed / [bathrooms] bath, [built-up] sqft.

May I know your budget range and whether you're looking to buy for own stay or investment? This helps me recommend the best match for you.

2 • Arranging a Viewing

Great, let's arrange a viewing! 🏡

I'm free on [date/time options]. Which slot works best for you?

Please bring along your IC for security registration at the guardhouse. See you soon!

3 • Post-Viewing Follow-Up

Hi [Buyer Name], thanks for viewing the unit today!

What did you think of the layout and condition? If you have any questions about pricing, loan eligibility, or the surrounding area, I'm happy to help. Let me know if you'd like to proceed with a booking. 🙏

4 • Booking Confirmation

Congratulations on your decision, [Buyer Name]! 🎉

To secure the unit, we'll need a booking fee of RM[amount]. Once received, I'll prepare the booking form and forward it to the owner/developer.

Next steps: booking → loan application → Sale & Purchase Agreement (SPA) signing.

5 • Loan Update Message

Hi [Buyer Name], quick update on your loan application:

Status: [Submitted / Approved / Pending documents]
Bank: [Bank Name]
Approved margin: [90% / 95% / 100%]

Let me know if the bank needs any additional documents from you — happy to assist!

6 • SPA Process Update

Hi [Buyer Name], here's where we are in the process:

✅ Booking done
✅ Loan approved
🔄 SPA drafted by lawyer — awaiting your signature
⏳ Next: down payment + stamp duty

I'll keep you updated every step of the way. Let me know if you have any questions!

7 • Asking for Referral

Hi [Buyer Name], it's been a pleasure helping you find your new home! 🏡

If you know any friends or family who are also looking to buy, rent, or sell, I'd really appreciate a referral. I'll make sure to take great care of them too. Thank you so much! 🙏
