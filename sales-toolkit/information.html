3 • Owner Listing Checklist

☐ Confirm ownership (title / SPA copy)
☐ Confirm asking price & flexibility
☐ Confirm vacant possession date
☐ Take photos / video walkthrough

☐ Confirm exclusive or open listing

4 • Loan Submission Document Checklist

☐ Copy of IC (front & back)
☐ Latest 3 months payslip
☐ Latest 3-6 months bank statement
☐ EPF statement (latest)
☐ Borang BE / EA form (latest year)
☐ CCRIS / CTOS report (if self-checked)
☐ Booking form / Letter of Offer
☐ Employment confirmation letter (if required)
☐ Business registration + 6 months bank statement (for self-employed)
